Before you start

Instructor questions

Do I need decent recording gear?

Audio matters, video mostly doesn't. A $60 USB microphone in a room with soft furnishings beats a $2,000 camera in a kitchen. Screen recordings are fine for anything software-related, and plenty of our best-rated courses are exactly that.

How long should a course be?

As long as the subject needs and no longer. Our highest-rated course is 4.5 hours. Padding a course to hit an arbitrary length is the single most common reason a first course gets mediocre reviews.

What gets a course rejected?

Unusable audio, content you don't have the rights to, previews that are all intro and no substance, and courses that are a funnel for something else you're selling. That's the whole list. We don't reject on subject matter or on your credentials.

Can I update a course after publishing?

Yes, and you should. Existing students get the update free and we email them about it, which reliably brings people back. Re-recording a section doesn't restart review unless the course changes substantially.

What if nobody buys it?

Then it costs you the time you spent, and nothing else. There's no listing fee and no minimum sales commitment. Roughly a third of courses here sell under 50 copies in their first year, which we'd rather say than hide.
