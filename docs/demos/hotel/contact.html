Asked often enough to answer here

Why can't I book one night on a Saturday?

In peak season a Saturday cannot be the first night of a stay, and Friday and Saturday arrivals need two nights (three in peak). It is deliberate: selling a single Saturday leaves the Friday and Sunday either side much harder to sell, so those nights are held for guests staying across the weekend.

The booking page tells you which rule applied and offers the nearest dates that work, rather than just saying no.

A room was available yesterday and isn't today

Availability is per night, and there are only two Observatory Penthouses and one Terrace Penthouse in the building. One booking on one night of your stay is enough to remove a room type from your results entirely.

If that happens, the results page names the night that blocked it, so you can shift by a day rather than starting again.

Is there a resort fee?

No. Wifi, the pool, the steam room, the sauna and the gym are included in every rate. The only additions to your room rate are the 14.5% occupancy tax and the $4.50 per night city levy, both of which are itemised before you enter your name.

Can I have a specific room?

You book a room type, not a room number, that is how hotels work, and anyone promising otherwise is guessing. Tell us what matters (high floor, quiet side, away from the lift) and reception will do what it can on the day. We will tell you honestly what we managed.

Children, cots and extra beds

Children stay free in the room's existing beds. Cots are free and available in every room type; an extra bed is $45 a night and only fits the Corner Deluxe, the Junior Suite and the penthouses.

The booking page will not offer you a room that cannot hold your party. A Harbour King takes two adults and one child, and it will say so rather than letting you book and find out.

Accessibility

Six rooms are step-free with roll-in showers: four Harbour Kings and two Quarter Twins, on floors six, eight and eleven. Lifts reach every floor including the eighteenth. The pool has a hoist.

The Long Room is step-free from the lobby. The harbour steps outside, unavoidably, are steps. There is a ramped route 80 metres east.
