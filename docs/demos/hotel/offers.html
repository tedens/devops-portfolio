Offers & rates

Three rates, and what they actually mean

No tiers, no members-only pricing, no countdown timers. Three rate plans that differ in one respect: what happens when your plans change.

Prices shown against each plan are for a sample two-night midweek stay in a Harbour King, so they compare like for like. Your own dates will differ , every night is priced separately.

Packages

Four things worth adding

Two nights, one dinner

Any two consecutive nights, with dinner for two in the Long Room on one of them. Three courses, not a set menu. Order what you want up to $75 a head.

From two nights · add at the desk

The slow Sunday

Sunday night with a 4pm checkout on the Monday, breakfast for two, and the baths to yourself before 9am. Our quietest night, priced accordingly.

Sunday arrivals · all year

Penthouse, properly

Either penthouse for three nights or more: airport transfers both ways, breakfast daily, and the butler from 7am. Included in the rate, not sold on top of it.

Three nights or more · automatic

The long stay

Seven nights or more takes 15% off the room rate and includes laundry twice a week. Useful if you are working here rather than visiting.

Seven nights or more · all room types

Rate conditions, in plain words

Flexible

Cancel or change free of charge until 6pm the day before you arrive. Nothing is taken until you check out.

After 6pm the day before, one night's room rate is charged. That is the only penalty, and it does not grow.

Advance purchase

18% off, charged in full at the time of booking, and not refundable, including if you don't turn up. Dates cannot be moved.

It is genuinely cheaper and genuinely inflexible. If there is any chance your plans will move, the flexible rate will cost you less overall.

Bed & breakfast

The flexible rate plus breakfast in the Long Room for two, at $32 a night for the pair. Breakfast on the door is $27 each, so it pays for itself with two people.

Cancellation is identical to the flexible rate.

Minimum stays and arrival dates

Friday and Saturday arrivals need two nights all year. In peak season they need three, and a peak Saturday cannot be the first night of a stay at all, that Saturday is held for guests already arriving on the Friday.

This is ordinary hotel revenue management, and the booking page says so in those words rather than just refusing the date.

Taxes and the levy

Occupancy tax is 14.5% of the room rate. The city levy is $4.50 per room per night. Both are shown as separate lines in the total before you enter a name, and neither is a resort fee. We do not charge one.
