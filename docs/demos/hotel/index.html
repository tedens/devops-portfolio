The building

Left alone where it mattered

The Lumen was the Harbour Board's shipping office, built in 1927 and empty for thirty-one years. We kept the terrazzo, the brass, the lift dials and the ridiculous ceiling heights, and put glass where the loading doors used to be.

What that means as a guest: the rooms are taller than they are new, the windows actually open on the lower floors, and the walls between rooms are 1927 concrete rather than 2021 plasterboard. You will not hear your neighbour.

Booking here

We show you the nights, not just a price

A hotel room is not free or taken. It is free or taken on each night. Most booking forms hide that and simply tell you “no availability”. Ours shows you every night of your stay, what each one costs, and exactly which night is the problem when there is one.

  • Every night of the stay priced separately, because Friday is not Tuesday
  • When a room is full on one night of four, we say which night
  • Minimum stays and closed arrival dates explained, not just enforced
  • Taxes and the city levy in the total before you give us a name
