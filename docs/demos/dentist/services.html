06. Emergency

In pain today?

We hold slots every weekday morning and afternoon. Call before 10am and we'll almost always see you the same day. Outside hours, the main line routes you to the on-call dentist.

Call (555) 014-2200 Request an urgent slot

Facial swelling, trouble swallowing or breathing, or trauma after a blow to the head are not dental emergencies. They're medical ones. Go to an emergency department.

Comfort options

Add any of these to any appointment

  • Comfort kit: blanket, headphones, eye pillow. Free, just ask.
  • Numbing before the needle: topical gel, then a warmed, slow injection. Standard, not an extra.
  • Nitrous oxide: $75. Wears off in minutes; you can drive home.
  • Oral sedation: $250. You'll be awake but hazy, and you'll need someone to drive you.
  • Break the visit up: we'll split longer treatment across shorter appointments at no extra cost.

Money questions

The awkward ones, answered

Are these prices real?

They're honest starting prices for a straightforward case. Complexity changes them. A filling across three surfaces costs more than one. You'll always get a written estimate with your insurance applied before we start, and we don't proceed past it without asking you first.

What happens if you find something mid-treatment?

We stop, tell you, and give you the number. Nothing extra gets done in the same visit unless you say yes to the new price. The only exception is something that would be unsafe to leave, and we'll still tell you before we do it.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Anything over $500 can go on a 0% in-house plan over six months with no credit check, or a longer third-party plan if you'd rather. Ask at the estimate stage.

Do you push treatment I don't need?

No, and you can hold us to it: every plan we write ranks work as urgent, soon, or watch. Plenty of plans are all watch. If a second opinion would help you feel better about a big number, we'll send your records over for free.
