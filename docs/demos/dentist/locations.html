Getting here

Practical things worth knowing

Arrive five minutes early

That's genuinely enough. Forms are done on your phone the night before, so there's nothing to fill in at the desk.

Parking is free

Riverside has its own lot. Northgate validates two hours of garage parking. Bring your ticket to the desk.

Both offices are step-free

Accessible restrooms at both. Tell us when you book if you need a wheelchair-accessible operatory or extra transfer time.

Need an interpreter, a support person in the room, or a quieter time of day? Say so when you book and we'll arrange it. There's no charge and no fuss.

Pick an office and a time

Takes about a minute. You'll get a text confirmation straight away.
