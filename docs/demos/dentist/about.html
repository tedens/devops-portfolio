About the practice

Started by someone who hated the dentist

Sherry Vance had four fillings before she was nine and didn't go back for a decade. She built the practice she wishes had existed, and eighteen years later, that's still the whole brief.

Our story

One chair above a bakery, in 2008

Sherry opened with a single operatory, a second-hand chair, and a rule she wrote on an index card and taped to the wall: tell them the price first, and stop when they raise their hand.

Both are still policy. So is the thing that came a year later, when a patient admitted she'd sat in the parking lot for twenty minutes before coming in: every new patient gets asked, plainly, how they feel about being here. The answer goes in the chart, and the visit is shaped around it.

We're two offices and fourteen people now. The index card is framed in the Riverside hallway, which is either sentimental or a warning to new hires, depending on who you ask.

How we work

Four things we won't bend on

The price comes first

A written estimate with insurance applied, before treatment. If we find something mid-appointment, we stop and re-quote rather than surprise you at the desk.

Your hand stops everything

Raise it and the drill stops, the suction comes out, and you get as long as you need. Nobody will sigh, and nobody will keep going "just to finish this bit".

We run on time

Appointments are booked with real durations, not optimistic ones. If we're running more than fifteen minutes late we call you before you leave the house.

"Watch it" is a real answer

Not every spot needs drilling today. Plans are ranked urgent / soon / watch, and plenty of them are entirely watch. We'd rather keep you for twenty years.

Eighteen years

How we got here

  • 2008

    One chair on Mill Street

    Sherry opens above what is now the bakery, with a second-hand chair and a receptionist who worked Tuesdays and Thursdays.

  • 2013

    The Riverside build-out

    Four operatories, a step-free entrance, and the parking lot that ended a decade of complaints about parking.

  • 2019

    Surgery comes in-house

    Dr. Oyelaran joins. Implants, surgical extractions and sedation stop being someone else's referral.

  • 2022

    Same-day crowns

    An in-office mill turns the two-visit crown into a two-and-a-half-hour one.

  • 2024

    Northgate opens

    A second office by the Line 4 station, for the third of our patients who were driving across town.

Around town

What we do when we're not drilling

  • Free sealant days every September for Brookfield Elementary and Northgate Primary, about 400 kids a year.
  • Sponsor of the Brookfield Riversiders under-11s, whose mouthguards we also make, for obvious reasons.
  • Veterans' clinic one Saturday a quarter, no charge, no paperwork.
  • Emergency fund: a slice of every membership fee covers urgent care for patients who genuinely can't pay.
