Why people stay

We built the practice around the nervous patient

Roughly a third of the people who walk in tell us they've avoided a dentist for years. So the whole visit is designed for them, and everyone else gets the benefit.

  • A stop signal that works. Raise your hand and we stop. Every time, no negotiation.
  • Numbing before the numbing. Topical gel first, then a slow, warmed anaesthetic.
  • The comfort kit. Blanket, headphones, eye pillow, and a playlist or podcast that's yours.
  • Price before treatment. A written estimate with your insurance applied, before anything starts.
  • Sedation options from nitrous through to oral sedation, discussed at the consult, never sprung on you.

Before you book

Common questions

Do you take my insurance?

We're in-network with most major PPO plans and we'll file the claim for you either way. Bring your card to the first visit and we'll verify coverage and show you the out-of-pocket number before we book any treatment.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our in-house membership plan is $29/month and covers two cleanings, exams, X-rays, and 20% off everything else. No deductible, no annual maximum, no claim forms.

I'm in pain right now. What do I do?

Call (555) 014-2200. We keep emergency slots open every weekday and will usually see you the same day. Outside office hours the line gives you the on-call number.

How much is a first visit?

$89 for new patients without insurance: full exam, digital X-rays, and a standard cleaning. With insurance it's typically covered at 100%. If the cleaning turns out to need more than a standard visit, we'll tell you the price before we do it, never after.

Can I bring my kids to my appointment?

Yes. Both offices have a small play corner in sight of the front desk, and we're happy to schedule the whole family back-to-back so it's one trip.
