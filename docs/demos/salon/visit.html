Wexford Row

Finding us, and what happens when you do

We're the green door between the bakery and the bike shop. There's no big sign. That's deliberate, and it's also why people walk past.

Parking

Two hours free on Alder Street, one street back. The Wexford Row bays are residents-only after 10am and they do check.

Tram & bus

Rowan Street tram stop is a four-minute walk. Buses 11 and 26 stop on Alder Street.

Access

One shallow step at the door with a portable ramp. Ring the bell or call and we'll bring it out. Ground floor throughout, accessible WC, and a basin that adjusts.

First time

What to expect

  1. Arrive five minutes early

    That's genuinely enough. There's a sofa, and the coffee is decent. If you're running late, call. We can usually still do something, but we'd rather know.

  2. Consultation with your hair dry

    Before the gown goes on. We'll look at your hair as it is, talk about what you want and what's realistic, and agree a price. Bring photos of what you like and what you don't.

  3. The appointment

    Tell us if you'd rather not chat. Genuinely, nobody minds, and there's a note in the system so you don't have to say it twice. Phone chargers at every chair.

  4. Before you leave

    We'll show you how it was dried and what we used. No pressure to buy the products; we'll happily tell you what's the same for half the price in a chemist.

Good to know

Practical things

Can I bring my children?

Yes. There's a box of things to do by the window and we'll keep an eye. We cut under-16s at $35 at any stylist level. Book it as a cut and finish and mention their age in the notes.

Do you take walk-ins?

Sometimes, for fringes and quick cuts, mostly early in the week. Call first rather than making the trip, with four chairs, a walk-in on a Saturday is unlikely.

I'm nervous about hairdressers

Say so in the notes when you book. We'll put you in the corner chair away from the window, skip the small talk, and check in before every stage rather than after. It's a common enough request that it's just how we work with some people.

What about allergies or scalp conditions?

Mention it at the consultation rather than in the booking notes, and we'll adjust the products and the patch test accordingly. If you've had a reaction to colour before, tell us what happened before you book anything.

Cancellation policy

Free to move or cancel up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge 50%. Appointments of two and a half hours or more take a $50 deposit that comes off the bill.
