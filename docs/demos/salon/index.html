Before you book

Common questions

Do I really need a patch test?

Yes, for anything involving colour or tint, including brow and lash tints, and including regulars. It's free, takes two minutes, and has to be at least 48 hours before. We can post you a kit if getting in is hard. This isn't us being difficult; reactions do happen and they can be serious.

How much will my colour actually cost?

The price list is the price list. Where hair is very long or very thick we sometimes need extra product, and we'll tell you that at the consultation and quote it before starting, never after. If you'd rather know in advance, book the free consult.

What if I don't like it?

Come back within fourteen days and we'll adjust it at no charge. That's for adjustments to what we did. A change of mind about the whole look is a new appointment, but we'll be fair about it.

Can I bring a photo?

Please do, and bring more than one. Photos of what you don't want are just as useful. We'll be straight with you about how close we can get on your hair, in the time booked.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free to move or cancel up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% of the booking, and for appointments over two and a half hours we take a $50 deposit up front that comes off the bill. Four chairs means one no-show is a real hole in the day.
