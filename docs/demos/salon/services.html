The fine print

Worth knowing

Patch tests

Required at least 48 hours before any colour, and before brow or lash tints. Free, two minutes, and required even if you've had the same colour elsewhere for years. We'll book it when we confirm, or post you a kit if getting in is difficult.

Deposits

Appointments of two and a half hours or more take a $50 deposit when we confirm, which comes off the final bill. It's refundable if you move or cancel more than 24 hours ahead.

Cancellations

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside that we charge 50% of the booking. Four chairs means one late cancellation is a real hole in the day, but if something genuinely went wrong, talk to us.

Adjustments

Fourteen days to come back and have us adjust what we did, free. That covers tone, a bit more off, a fringe that's sitting oddly. A change of mind about the whole look is a new appointment.

Under-16s and students

Under-16 cuts are $35 at any level. Student discount of 15% on Tuesdays and Wednesdays with a valid card, excluding colour services over $150.
