The short version

We make about twelve things, properly.

No drops, no seasonal churn, no markdown calendar. The same twelve pieces get re-cut when the cloth improves and stay on the site the rest of the time. If something sells out we make more of it, which sounds obvious and apparently isn't.

Everything is sewn in one factory outside Porto that we've used since the first run. The cloth comes from mills in Huddersfield, Hawick and Okayama, all named on the product page, because "premium fabric" means nothing.

How we make things

Repairs

Free for life on everything we make. Send it back, we fix it, we post it to you. Seams, buttons, moth holes, a blown knee.

Hemming

Trousers ship unhemmed at 82 cm. Tell us your inseam at checkout and we'll do it before it leaves, at no cost.

Returns

Sixty days, worn or not, as long as it's clean. Prepaid label in the box. No restocking fee, no form asking why.

Sizing

Flat measurements on every product page, in centimetres and inches, taken from the actual garment rather than a spec sheet.
