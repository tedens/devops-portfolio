How it works

Four steps, none of them a phone call

  1. Work out the size

    Add your furniture to the planner. It draws the unit to scale and tells you what fits, what doesn't, and why.

  2. Reserve it free

    We hold the unit for seven days. No card, no deposit, and you can cancel by replying to the email.

  3. Move in

    Sign at the office, get your gate code, and use our trolleys and loading bay for as long as you need them.

  4. Leave when you're done

    Ten days' notice on a monthly unit. We refund the part-month you don't use, the same way we charged for it.

What you pay on day one

A 10 × 10 climate-controlled unit, moving in mid-month.

The first month is charged by the day. Move in on the 22nd and you pay for nine days, not a month. Every price on this site works that way, and the sum is shown before you commit.

The site

What you're actually renting

Climate controlled

The two indoor buildings hold 55–78°F and under 60% humidity all year. That's what protects paper, timber, electronics and upholstery.

Drive-up rows

Back a van right up to the roller door. No corridors, no lift, no carrying. Best for tools, stock and anything heavy.

Gate, code and cameras

Your own gate code, logged on entry and exit. 34 cameras, lit to the fence line, and a manager on site six days a week.

Your lock, your key

You fit the only lock and keep the only key. We don't hold a copy, and nobody opens your unit without you or a court order.
