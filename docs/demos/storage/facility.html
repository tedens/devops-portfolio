Moving in

What the first hour looks like

  1. Park at the bay, not at the unit

    The covered loading bay is the fastest way in for anything in Building A or B. Trolleys and flat-bed carts live there and are free.

  2. Sign, and get your code

    Ten minutes at the office. Bring photo ID and, if you're using your own insurance, proof of the policy.

  3. Fit your lock

    Units are handed over open. Fit your own lock. Nobody, us included, has a key to it.

  4. Load the back wall first

    Heaviest and least-wanted at the back, an aisle down one side, and label the side of the box, not the top.

Things people wish they'd known

  • A 5 ft unit has a 3 ft swing door. A sofa, a pallet and most appliances need a 10 ft unit with a roller.
  • Upper floor is 12% cheaper and involves a lift. Fine to store and leave, annoying to visit weekly.
  • Drive-up is not climate controlled. Anything with paper, timber or a screen belongs indoors.
  • Boxes on top of the furniture is the difference between a 10 × 10 and a 10 × 20.

Moving between units is free. If the one you took turns out to be wrong, tell the office and we'll swap you. You pay the new rate from the day you move, prorated, and nothing for the swap itself.
