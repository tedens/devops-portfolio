What the options mean

Climate controlled

Held at 55–78°F and under 60% humidity. Worth it for paper, photographs, timber furniture, electronics, instruments, upholstery and anything with a screen. Adds 22% to the rate.

Drive-up

Park at the roller door and unload straight in, no corridor and no lift. Best for heavy or awkward things, trade stock and vehicles. Not climate controlled, and adds 10%.

Upper floor

Goods lift from the loading bay, then a short corridor. Identical unit, 12% cheaper, one more stage to carry. Fine if you're storing and leaving; less fine if you'll visit weekly.
