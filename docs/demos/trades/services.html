The small print

What's included, and what isn't

Always included

  • Testing and certification for anything notifiable
  • Registering the work on your behalf
  • Making good. Chases filled, dust sheeted, floor swept
  • Two-year guarantee on our workmanship

Priced separately

  • Plastering and decorating beyond making good
  • Lifting and relaying tiled or engineered floors
  • Fittings you want us to supply, at cost plus 10%
  • Scaffold or tower hire where a ladder won't do

Prices are for properties inside our coverage area. Older houses and awkward access can add to a job. We'll tell you at the survey and quote it before starting, never after.
