Why a range

The things nobody can see down a phone line

  1. What's already in the walls

    Rubber-insulated cable from the fifties turns a two-day job into a four-day one. We won't know until we lift a socket, which takes ten minutes at a survey and nothing at all over the phone.

  2. How the floors are built

    Suspended timber is straightforward. Screed, engineered board or a tiled floor that can't be lifted means surface trunking or a longer route, and that's a real cost difference.

  3. Where the supply comes in

    An EV charger on the same wall as the meter is an afternoon. One at the far end of the garden, past a driveway, is not.

If someone quotes you a firm price without looking, be careful. Either they've padded it heavily, or there's a conversation coming halfway through the job. We'd rather show you the range and then survey it.
