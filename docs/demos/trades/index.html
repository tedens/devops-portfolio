How the booking works

Windows, not times

Any electrician promising you 9:15am is guessing. We give you a morning or an afternoon, we call half an hour before we set off, and if we're running late you hear it from us rather than wondering.

  • Morning 8am – 12pm, or afternoon 12pm – 5pm.
  • Bigger jobs take the whole day and we book it as one block, not two.
  • A rewire takes two consecutive days minimum, and we'll say so before you commit.
  • Evenings available in the core area at an out-of-hours rate, stated up front.

Before you book

Common questions

Do you charge to come out?

No call-out charge anywhere we cover, including the Tuesday/Thursday fringe area. For fixed-price jobs the price on the list is the price. For anything surveyed, the survey itself is free and carries no obligation.

How long will you be here?

The booking tells you before you commit, one arrival window, a full day, or two days. We'd rather over-book the time and leave early than turn a one-day job into three visits.

What if it turns out to be bigger than expected?

We stop and tell you the new number before carrying on. Nothing extra gets done on the assumption you'll agree. If it's something we should have spotted at the survey, that's on us and the quote stands.

Is the work certified?

Anything notifiable gets a certificate and is registered on your behalf, which matters when you sell. You'll get the paperwork by email within two working days, and we keep a copy for as long as we're trading.

Do I need to be home the whole time?

Someone over 18 needs to be there for the whole window. We can't let ourselves in or leave the property unattended with circuits open. If that's awkward, say so in the booking notes and we'll work something out.
